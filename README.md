* Your core user: Beginners

Search 
* What: The user searches for specific body parts and receives curated, beginner-friendly exercise recommendations.
* When: While building or updating their weekly workout plan.
* Why: Beginners often don't know exercise names or proper movement techniques.
* Where: Inside the Search Bar at the top of the Explore Tab.
* How: By typing terms into the search bar (e.g., "Legs", "Shoulders", "Chest").

View All
* What: The user can browse the complete library of beginner exercises, pre-made 4-day templates, and their full scheduled weekly routine.
* When: On rest days or before starting a workout session.
* Why: Gives beginners a clear view of all available options without forcing them to guess.
* Where: On the Exercise Library Screen or the Weekly Calendar Overview Page.
* How: By tapping the "Library" icon on the bottom navigation bar.

Create
* What: The user builds their weekly workout schedule by selecting workouts from the search/library and assigning them to their 4-day calendar.
* When: At the start of a new week or during initial setup.
* Why: Enforces a strict 4-day limit to prevent beginners from overtraining or burning out.
* Where: 
* How: 

Edit
* What: The user adjusts scheduled workout days or swaps out specific exercises.
* When: When daily plans change, equipment is occupied, or an exercise feels too difficult.
* Why: Keeps the routine flexible so missing a single day doesn't ruin their motivation.
* Where: Directly on the the Daily Schedule Modal.
* How: By tapping the "Edit Plan" or "Swap Exercise" button on a scheduled day.

Dashboard / Homepage
* What: The user views today's scheduled workout, weekly day progress (e.g., "2 of 4 Days Completed"), total estimated calories burned, and a progress summary.
* When: First thing upon opening the app—before starting a workout or checking achievements.
* Why: Gives instant clarity, keeps them accountable, and ensures they stay on track toward their goal.
* Where: On the Main Landing Screen (the default tab when opening the app).
* How: By opening the app or tapping the "Home" icon on the bottom navigation bar.
